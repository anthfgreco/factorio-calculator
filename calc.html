What does clicking on an item's icon in the "Factory" tab do?

It omits the dependencies of that item from the solution. The intent behind this feature is to model the case of building a factory when you are shipping in one or more ingredients from elsewhere. For example, if you are making an advanced circuit factory and want to ship in electronic circuits, and want to see the remaining copper cable requirements on their own.

Clicking on the icon a second time will restore its dependencies.

How do multiple production locations work?

Use the Locations control above to choose where recipes may run. Selecting multiple locations creates one shared production plan using recipes that are possible on at least one selected location. The Factory table shows the possible location or locations for each active recipe. The calculator does not yet assign ambiguous recipes to a planet or include rocket and platform transport costs. Machine electric-power totals exclude beacon buildings because the calculator does not assume a physical beacon-sharing layout.

How is quality handled?

The calculator validates whether quality modules are allowed in the selected machine and recipe, includes their electricity effect, and prevents them from being placed in beacons. Speed modules retain their negative quality modifier, but the calculator does not yet split production into quality tiers or calculate recycling-based upcycling yields.

Why did the calculator reject my plan?

The Factory tab now explains when no enabled recipe can produce an item or when a cyclic, multi-output recipe system is infeasible. Enable a compatible recipe or location, choose an alternate recipe, adjust resource priority, or treat an input as imported by clicking its item icon.

Features of this calculator include:

  • Proper handling of oil products. Obtaining numbers for these recipes can be tricky, as several of the items involved may be produced from multiple recipes.
  • Support for Factorio 2.1 and Space Age production locations, recipes, recycling, machines, and modules.
  • Compatibility-aware modules and beacons, including Factorio 2.0+ beacon profiles.
  • Factory summaries, in-row alternate recipe selection, and shareable URL state.
  • Support for the mining productivity bonus, introduced in 0.15.
  • Support for "expensive" mode.
  • Arbitrary numerical precision. Calculations are performed using bigint rationals, so errors from floating-point calculations should not be an issue.
  • Support for multiple simultaneous outputs.
  • May display rates per second, minute, or hour.
