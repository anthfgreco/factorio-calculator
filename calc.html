Factorio Calculator

A maintained fork of Kirk McDonald’s calculator for planning production chains from starter factories through Space Age megabases. It supports Factorio 2.1 recipes, locations, recycling, machines, modules, beacons, productivity restrictions, and shareable calculator URLs. The project is licensed under the Apache License 2.0 and developed openly on GitHub.

FAQ

How do I treat an ingredient as imported?

Click its item icon in the Factory table. Its upstream dependencies are removed; click again to restore them.

What do progression presets change?

Presets quickly select sensible locations, modules, beacons, belts, and mining productivity for a stage of the game. They never change your production targets, and every setting remains editable.

How do production locations work?

Select one location, or Shift-click to combine locations. Combined plans share materials; recipe assignment and transport costs are not modeled yet.

How is quality handled?

Module compatibility, power effects, and speed-module quality penalties are validated. Quality tiers and recycling upcycling are not yet solved as separate item flows.

What do the factory totals include?

Machines to place rounds each recipe up. Machine power is the electricity used by those machines.

How do alternate recipes affect the result?

Use the recipe control in a Factory row or the Recipes section in Settings. When several solutions are valid, the Resources tab controls which inputs the solver prefers.

Why was my plan rejected?

The plan may lack an enabled production path or contain an infeasible cyclic or multi-output system. Check locations, recipes, priorities, and imported inputs.
